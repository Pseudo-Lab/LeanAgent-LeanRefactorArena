# P02 LESSONS

## R1
- `solve_by_elim* only [...]` (all goals at once) is marginally cheaper than `all_goals apply_rules only [...]` (H 326k -> 324k).
- Dropping `only` or reordering lemmas (nil/refl first) costs 4-5 J; keep `Sublist.append` first when it's in the list.

## R2
- `apply_rules (config := {symm := false})` / `exfalso := false` explodes HB (~1600k); defaults are best here.
- Adding extra unfolding (`withReducible`-style tweaks) didn't help.

## R3
- Structural split first (`<;> repeat' apply List.Sublist.append`) then closing leaves with a small lemma set lowers H ~12% (326k -> 288k), outweighing +2 tokens.
- After the split, `solve_by_elim*` beats `all_goals apply_rules`; keeping `Sublist.append` in the closer list hurts.

## R4
- `all_goals first | assumption | apply List.nil_sublist | apply List.Sublist.refl` beats `solve_by_elim*` as a leaf closer (H 288k -> 252k): fixed cheap tactics avoid the sbe search overhead.
- `first`-chain ordering matters: put the most frequent/cheapest-to-fail alternative first (assumption > nil_sublist > refl); refl first costs +60k.

## R5-R6
- `repeat'` already acts on all goals, so `all_goals repeat' ...` -> `repeat' ...` saves a token at equal H.
- `rintro -` is required: keeping the hypothesis (`intro _`) turns the IHs into implications and breaks `assumption`/refl.

## R7-R8 (stop: 2 rounds without meaningful gain)
- Per-leaf profile: each failed `apply List.Sublist.append` on a leaf costs ~6k HB; that's the floor for this approach.
- Unfolding the instance projection (`unfold/dsimp/simp only [HasVarsPure.getVars]`) saves at most ~2k HB, which doesn't pay for the extra tokens.
